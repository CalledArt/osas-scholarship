# Support

## STEM-only scholarships use CvsuPrograms::STEM, taken from DOST-SEI's list
A posting with stem_courses_only admits (policy), lists (Scholarship::availableTo) and emails only students whose registered course is in CvsuPrograms::STEM. That list is the CvSU programs whose names exactly match the "S&T Undergraduate Scholarship Priority Fields" in the DOST-SEI 2026 brochure (science-scholarships.ph). Near matches (Agricultural Entrepreneurship, Exercise and Sports Sciences, BSEd/BEEd/BTLEd without a recorded major) are deliberately excluded; recheck against the new brochure each year. The DOST preset in ScholarshipPresets turns the limit on; never check eligibility by scholarship title. Every posting has a ScholarshipType (Government or Academic).
